Made as a visual companion to Hampton Sides’s The Wide Wide Sea. The fine line follows the voyage’s principal turns rather than every logged position; the dashed section begins after Cook’s death. Artwork titles keep period spellings such as Owyhee, Otaheite, and Kamtschatka; map labels use modern names where practical. Some oils were finished in London years after the sketches.
